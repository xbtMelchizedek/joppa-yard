# Legal boundary

Joppa Yard is an original work. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from Rockstar Games, Take-Two, Sony, PlayStation, or Grand Theft Auto.

We do not ship, accept, or use commercial game ROMs, console firmware, or proprietary art, audio, maps or trademarks. We do not host or wrap Grand Theft Auto, FiveM, RedM, or a public remote-play queue on a real console.

FiveM/RedM, even where Rockstar authorizes community servers for a covered game, is the wrong rail for this project: the Creator Platform License Agreement (10 Sept 2026) § 3.1(8) prohibits facilitating the use, sale or promotion of cryptocurrencies or crypto assets. Paying for inference in FLOP on such a server is out of scope.

A longer source-cited note, including why a PS5 emulator or a public "play GTA 6 in the browser" queue is off limits, lives in the author's research log `FLOP_AGENT_GAME_WORLD_2026-11-24.md`. This file is the in-repo reminder.
